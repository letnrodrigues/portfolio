HR Tech RPGs

PushStart Studio, Kolab
Technologies: Construct 3, JavaScript
Platform: Web

PushStart developed and released multiple RPG-style games for a variety of purposes, including assessment tools for recruitment processes, interactive experiences introducing candidates to companies and their cultures, and themed experiences for special events such as the FIFA World Cup.

Clients included Braskem, IEL, Ambev, and PepsiCo. A selection of the games was released in multiple languages, with one title available in four.

🔗 Braskem Game, article by PushStart

Contributions & Responsibilities

I worked on PushStart RPGs for over 4 years, progressing from developing self-contained features such as minigames to implementing major gameplay systems and eventually mastering the codebase. One of my first major contributions was developing a new game update featuring an Audio Recorder mechanic used to evaluate approximately 3,000 candidates in a recruitment process.

I eventually took ownership of the codebase, leading efforts to develop a modular, reusable architecture that could be adapted across different projects and requirements.

My work covered a broad range of systems and features, including navigation, player and quest data management, dialogue, animation, scene and map construction, photo mode, character creation and customization, UI and HUDs, game flow, theming, subtitles, sound implementation, and minigames.

I also worked on integrating game systems with internal development tools, improving both to streamline production. This included integrating the quest system with our quest editor, reducing programmer involvement in certain projects from weeks to days.

Selected Contributions

  • Minigames: developed self-contained minigames for both entertainment and assessment purposes.

  • Dialogue System: Implemented dialogue functionality supporting branching choices, character animations, items, modals, and conditional logic. Also contributed to a graphical dialogue editor to facilitate dialogue creation and editing.

  • Quest System: expanded and improved an existing system responsible for managing quest progression and game world state. Implemented additional gameplay flows and handled new state-related scenarios to support evolving project requirements.

  • Customization Architecture: designed and implemented reusable customization systems that could be adapted to different project requirements, reducing the need for project-specific programming and facilitating content configuration.

  • Codebase Refactoring: led efforts to improve code quality, maintainability, and usability. This included modularizing existing systems, optimizing memory usage through improved asset management, and addressing technical issues involving HUD scaling and animation timing.

  • Cross-Team Work: worked closely with the art team to define customization possibilities and technical constraints early in production, ensuring that designs were feasible to implement and reducing feedback cycles caused by implementation limitations.
