HR Tech Gamified Stories

PushStart Studio, Kolab
Technologies: Construct 3, JavaScript
Platform: Web

HR Tech Gamified Stories is an interactive web experience inspired by Instagram Stories that delivers customizable, gamified experiences for recruitment, onboarding, cultural fit assessments, training, marketing, quizzes and more.

Over four years, hundreds of products were delivered to dozens of companies, including Ambev, Braskem, PepsiCo, Itaú, Raízen, and Bradesco. The platform reached thousands of users across multiple Latin American countries.

Contributions & Responsibilities

Worked on the project for approximately four years, progressively taking ownership of its architecture, codebase, and development processes. Over time, became the primary technical reference for the project, guiding technical decisions, coordinating development efforts, and mentoring other developers.

I have also customized and handcrafted tens of products for our clients.

  • Features Development: implemented modular, customizable systems for all features, including tutorials, subtitles, interactive screens, minigames, and multiple input methods, ensuring easy adaptation to individual client requirements.

  • Refactorating & Optimization: Led a major refactoring effort focused on memory usage, asset optimization, loading times, FPS performance, and code standardization. Achieved approximately 70% lower memory usage and significantly reduced build sizes and mandatory download requirements, lowering bandwidth consumption, storage demands, and server load. Introduced a more modular architecture that enabled junior developers to contribute more effectively. Also improved collaboration with designers by establishing technical guidelines that helped translate designs into implementation-ready assets.

  • Self-Interview Feature: Worked on a feature allowing candidates to record video or audio responses to interview questions. Focused on maximizing cross-device compatibility and provided post-launch support, including log analysis and troubleshooting to ensure a reliable and fair candidate experience.

  • Development Tools Integration: refactored and extended the application architecture to support customization through internal tools, enabling non-developers to configure projects independently. AAlso introduced the ability to deploy hotfixes and update content directly in response to client feedback, without requiring a full development cycle.

  • Bugfixing & Maintenance: investigated and resolved bugs across the application, ensuring stability and reliability throughout continuous development and deployment.

  • Technical Leadership & Team Management: planned and distributed development tasks using Kanban-inspired workflows, conducted code reviews, mentored developers, and made technical decisions regarding the codebase. Coordinated with other team leads, monitored progress and deadlines, and oversaw deliveries to ensure quality and timely completion.

  • Cross-Team Management: coordinated with QA and design teams to align workflows with technical constraints. Conducted knowledge-sharing sessions for designers on topics such as image optimization and memory usage, helping incorporate technical considerations earlier in the design process.
